01 // ABOUT

BUILT AROUND
ACTIVITY.

SPΞCTRΞ is for players who want an active guild with people they can actually play with.

We organize Dark Sea expeditions, dragon hunts, reputation campaigns, island claiming, PvP sessions, and other group events.

The guild has a vigilante identity, but we are not built around starting unnecessary conflicts. Most of the time, the goal is progression, events, and helping members improve.

When conflict does happen, we prefer to handle it as a group rather than leaving members to deal with it alone.
